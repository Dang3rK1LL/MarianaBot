# Decision

Should I launch a subscription service that helps independent local businesses
manage repeat customer appointments and follow-up?

# Scope and constraints

- Geography: Hungary.
- First customers: identify and compare two narrow customer segments.
- Founder: one person, part time, with software development skills.
- Pilot budget: propose alternatives; do not assume I have committed any spending.
- Time horizon: a decision and testable pilot plan within six weeks.
- Prefer a small paid pilot before building a full product.

# Required output

Compare demand, competitors, willingness to pay, acquisition methods, operational
work, unit economics, and reasons the idea could fail. Produce an action plan with
owners, dependencies, relative deadlines, decision thresholds and clear kill criteria.

Separate sourced facts from assumptions. Do not invent interviews or market data.
Specify which customer evidence I need to collect and how it could disprove the idea.
